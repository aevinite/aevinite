# Aevinite

**Software that runs real businesses.** Aevinite is a software company based in **Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India**. Rishi Nakrani (Founder) and Jenish Patel (Co-Founder) started it on **28 July 2024** under the name *Infinite*, and renamed it **Aevinite** on 16 June 2026.

We build custom business software, SaaS products, websites and apps, 3D web experiences and AI automation.

## What we build

- **Custom systems:** business software built around how a company actually works
- **SaaS products:** for example *Aevidine*, a complete restaurant platform with QR ordering, kitchen screens, billing and owner reports
- **Websites and apps:** fast, modern, mobile-first
- **3D and interactive web experiences**
- **Process automation and AI integration**

## The name

*Aevinite* comes from **Aevum**, a dimension of existence with a beginning but no end, and echoes **Infinite**, the company's original name. *The beginning with no end.*
